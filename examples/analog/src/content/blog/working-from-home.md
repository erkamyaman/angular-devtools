# Working from home, a year in

![](/blog/home-office.webp)

A year of remote work taught us which gear earns its place.

## Kept

- A good chair, more than anything else
- Noise-cancelling headphones for calls
- A bag that fits the laptop and a charger, nothing more

## Dropped

- Second monitors for everyone. One large screen was enough for most of us.
- Webcam lights. A window works better.

## Would buy again

The **Messenger Bag**. It survived a year of commuting and still looks new.
