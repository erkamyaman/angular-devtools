# Getting started

Welcome to Analog Shop. This guide takes you from an empty cart to your first order.

## Create an account

Go to [Create account](/register), enter your name and email, and accept the terms. You can also check out as a guest.

## Find a product

Browse [all products](/products), or filter by category: audio, desk or bags. Use the search box to find a product by name.

## Place an order

Add products to the cart, open the [cart](/cart) and choose **Checkout**. Orders placed before noon ship the same day.
