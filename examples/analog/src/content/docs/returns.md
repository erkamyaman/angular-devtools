# Returns

You can return anything within 30 days.

## How to return

1. Open your order in the [dashboard](/dashboard).
2. Choose **Return** and print the free label.
3. Drop the parcel at any carrier point.

## Refunds

We refund to the original payment method within three working days of receiving the parcel.
